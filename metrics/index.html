Magnitude only: |Q| ≈ √(S² − P²). The firmware cannot tell leading from lagging, so the estimate is drawn upward without assigning a sign. For non-sinusoidal loads, this also includes distortion—not just reactive power. This is a power diagram, not reactance in ohms.

Both axes use the same numeric scale. W is the useful comparison for reducing energy use; VA also includes the reactive component. The chart keeps up to 30 minutes in this tab only; reloading clears it. Pauses and failed reads leave gaps. Imported energy comes from the board's counter and resets when it reboots. This is a diagnostic view of your homemade sensor, not a billing meter.
