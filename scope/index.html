Both channels use ADC-scale millivolts, not calibrated mains volts or amps. Updated firmware corrects current polarity around its DC bias; uncorrected readings remain available from the board at /samples?raw=1. Each has its own vertical scale. Matching sample indexes are roughly time-aligned; there is no precise timebase. A snapshot may contain old data if acquisition has stopped. The board must be running firmware with /samples support.
